Your Currency Converter Pro account

Request account deletion

Email lofuz@proton.me with the subject “Currency Converter Pro account deletion request”.

What to include

Say that you want your Currency Converter Pro account deleted and tell us the sign-in method you use. You can send the request from the email address associated with your sign-in account, but an email address alone is not proof of account ownership.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, receipts, card details or other payment credentials. We will explain any additional steps needed to verify ownership securely.

What happens next

We review the request and verify that you own the account before changing account data. We then explain the account and synced settings affected, any records that need to remain, and the outcome of the request.

This page starts an email request. It does not perform instant automated deletion. In the app, Account > Request account deletion opens these email-request instructions.

Information covered by the request

The request covers your app account, the link to your sign-in identity and synced converter settings, including favorite currency pairs, named trip settings and fee preferences. Local settings and cached data may also remain on your device; removing app data is separate from a server account request.

Some billing ownership, trial-use and security records need to be retained to protect account ownership and prevent abuse. These records are not automatically erased by an account deletion request. We will explain any retained information and the reason for retaining it during the review. See the privacy policy for the information used by the app and its service providers.

Cancel a subscription separately

Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription. If you purchased through Google Play, manage renewal in Google Play using the account that made the purchase. See subscription support for instructions. RevenueCat Test Store purchases in the current development build are separate from real Google Play subscriptions.

You can request account deletion by email even if you can no longer open the app. Tell us if you have also lost access to your sign-in account so we can discuss ownership verification.
