Privacy policy

This policy explains how Currency Converter Pro uses information in the app and on this information website.

Updated 14 October 2026

Contact

Currency Converter Pro is provided by Lofuz Solutions.

For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact lofuz@proton.me. Please do not send passwords or payment credentials.

Sign-in and your account

The app uses Google sign-in through Firebase Authentication. Google and Firebase process sign-in information, which can include your Google account identifier, email address and profile information. The app receives an authenticated session and uses a stable internal account identifier to connect your settings and access records to the correct account.

Our account service stores the sign-in identifier, internal account identifier and account creation time in Google Cloud Firestore. An email address alone does not establish ownership of an app account.

Converter settings and trips

Your home currency, selected currency pair, favorite pairs, additional destination currencies, fee adjustment and named trip settings are saved on your device. When account sync is available, these settings are also sent to our service and stored in Cloud Firestore for your account, so they can be retrieved across sessions and devices. Trip settings include the trip name, currency pair and fee adjustment; avoid putting sensitive information in trip names.

Subscriptions and access

When billing is enabled, RevenueCat receives your internal app account identifier and purchase information to manage and verify subscription status. Our service checks subscription evidence and stores account-linked access status, renewal and expiry information, purchase ownership references and any trial-use history. These records help grant access to the right account and prevent misuse or repeat trial redemption.

The current Android development billing flow uses RevenueCat's Test Store. Test Store purchases are separate from Google Play subscriptions. If a purchase is made through a real app store, that store also processes the purchase under its own policies.

Camera and price recognition

The optional Android scanner asks for camera permission when you use it. It uses CameraX and Google's bundled ML Kit text recognition to process captures on your device. Images and recognized text are held in memory for review and are released when the scan is cleared or closed. The scanner does not save captures to your photo library or upload images or recognized text to our servers or to Google for recognition.

ML Kit separately collects device and app information, diagnostic information and usage or performance metrics, and sends these to Google to operate, maintain and improve its services and detect misuse. On-device recognition does not mean the SDK sends no other data. See ML Kit's privacy information and Android data disclosures.

You can decline camera permission and enter amounts manually, or change the permission in Android settings.

Rates, local storage and service operation

The app retrieves reference rates and historical rate data from our service. The service obtains European Central Bank reference data via Frankfurter. Rate requests use your authenticated account to check access; they do not send camera images to the rate provider.

Local device storage holds converter settings, appearance choices, cached rate data and access information. Google/Firebase and RevenueCat also handle the session and service data needed by their components. Our service keeps account-related request counts to limit abuse. Network and hosting providers can process technical information such as IP addresses, request times and diagnostic logs to deliver and secure these services.

Website and email

This website has no data-entry forms, analytics scripts, advertising, remote fonts or site-set cookies. GitHub Pages hosts it; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are processed to respond to your request. The contact mailbox uses Proton Mail; your own email provider also processes the message. See Proton's privacy policy.

Retention and deletion requests

Account information and synced settings remain stored while they are needed to provide the account service. Billing ownership, trial-use history and security records are durable records used to protect account ownership and prevent abuse; requesting account deletion does not automatically erase these records.

Contact us to request account deletion or ask about your stored information. Requests require ownership verification and individual review. We will explain which information can be removed and which records need to be retained, and why. This is an email request process, not instant automated deletion.

Service providers

The services described above include Google (Google sign-in, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, hosting and ML Kit), RevenueCat (subscription verification), GitHub (this website) and Proton (support email). Their processing is also covered by their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
