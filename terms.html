Using the app

Terms of use

Currency Converter Pro helps you compare currency amounts and reference-rate trends.

Updated 14 October 2026

Reference rates and estimates

Conversions and trends use indicative reference-rate data. They are estimates, not transaction quotes or financial advice. A bank, card provider or exchange desk may use a different rate or apply fees. Check the rate and total cost with the provider before making a payment or exchange.

Cached rates may be older than the latest available data. Check the date shown in the app. The data source is the European Central Bank, via Frankfurter; Currency Converter Pro calculates conversions, cross-rates and historical change summaries.

Check scanned prices

Text recognition can misread an amount, decimal separator or currency. Review and correct a scanned result before using it. You can always enter an amount manually.

Accounts and access

Sign in with your own account and keep access to it secure. Account access and saved settings are linked to your authenticated identity. Do not use the service to interfere with other accounts or bypass access checks.

Features that need account verification or current data can depend on network availability. Contact support if sign-in, sync or access cannot be verified.

Purchases and subscriptions

Where a subscription is offered, review the price, billing period and renewal details shown by the purchase service before confirming. Manage or cancel a store subscription through the store account used to buy it. A restore action checks existing purchase access; it does not create a new purchase or transfer ownership between app accounts.

The current Android development billing flow uses RevenueCat's Test Store, which is separate from Google Play. For a subscription actually purchased through Google Play, follow the Google Play subscription instructions. Purchase, cancellation and refund requests are subject to the relevant store process and your applicable rights.

Uninstalling the app or requesting account deletion does not cancel a store subscription. See support for help.

Your information and requests

The privacy policy explains account information, synced settings, camera processing and service providers. To request account deletion, follow the account deletion instructions.
