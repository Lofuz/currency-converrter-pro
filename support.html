A way to get help

Support

For Currency Converter Pro questions, email lofuz@proton.me.

Tell us what happened

Include your device model, Android version, app version if available, the action you tried and any error message. For sign-in or account questions, tell us which sign-in method you use. Share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Do not send your password, sign-in codes, purchase receipts, card details or private camera images.

Conversion, rates and sync

Check the rate date shown in the app and your internet connection. If settings have not synced, keep the app open and try again when connected. A bank or exchange desk may use a different rate and fees, so its quote can differ from the app's estimate.

Price scanner

Check that the app has camera permission in Android settings. Use clear lighting and review the detected amount and currency. If recognition is uncertain, enter the amount manually.

Manage or cancel a subscription

If you bought a subscription through Google Play, open Google Play with the account used for the purchase, choose Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, select the subscription and choose Cancel subscription. See Google's subscription help for current instructions and troubleshooting.

The current Android development build uses RevenueCat's Test Store. Its test purchases are separate from Google Play and will not appear in your Google Play subscriptions. Email support about a Test Store issue.

Uninstalling the app, signing out or requesting account deletion does not cancel a real store subscription. Manage renewal with the store separately.

Restore access

If your build offers Restore purchases, sign in to the app account associated with the purchase and use that action. Restoration checks an existing purchase; it cannot transfer it to a different account. If access remains unavailable, email support with the error message and whether you used Test Store or Google Play.

Account deletion and privacy

Follow the account deletion request instructions. For other questions about your information, read the privacy policy or email the same support address.
